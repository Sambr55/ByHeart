## Graça

Up the hill from Alfama: viewpoints locals actually use, and the 28 rattling past.

### O Pitéu da Graça — tasca

- **Where:** Largo da Graça 95-96
- **Why:** A family tasca on the square that has fed the neighbourhood for decades — at lunch it fills with locals over fish and grilled meat, and the house dish is bacalhau à Pitéu.
- **Line:** *Boa tarde, tem mesa para dois ao almoço?* — Good afternoon, do you have a table for two for lunch?
- **Meet:** The waiters who run the floor, and the regulars at the next table
- **Tip:** Book, or arrive right at opening. Said to close Saturday evening and Sunday.
- **Room:** `lisbon_tasca`
- **Unconfirmed:** Hours from Time Out; no 2025–26 confirmation.
- **Sources:** https://www.timeout.pt/lisboa/pt/restaurantes/piteu-da-graca · https://culinarybackstreets.com/?p=18721
- [x] verified (accepted by Sam, 2026-11-03)

### A Tasca do Jaime — venue

- **Where:** Rua da Graça 91
- **Why:** A small neighbourhood tasca where, on weekend afternoons, anyone can stand up and sing fado vadio — older regulars with a glass of red, and the room going silent for each voice.
- **Line:** *Um copo de tinto e um pastel de bacalhau, por favor.* — A glass of red and a codfish cake, please.
- **Meet:** Jaime behind the counter, the house guitarists, and whoever sings next
- **Tip:** Weekend afternoons from about 16:00. When someone says 'silêncio, que se vai cantar o fado', stop talking.
- **Room:** `lisbon_fado`
- **Unconfirmed:** Fado schedule differs between sources; no listing dated 2025–26.
- **Sources:** https://www.timeout.pt/lisboa/pt/restaurantes/tasca-do-jaime · https://lifecooler.com/artigo/atividades/tasca-do-jaime/411829 · https://devourtours.com/blog/free-fado-lisbon/
- [x] verified (accepted by Sam, 2026-11-03)

### Damas — bar

- **Where:** Rua da Voz do Operário 60
- **Why:** A bar, kitchen and gig room in an old bakery — the ovens are still in the music room — where the neighbourhood's young crowd comes for small concerts, DJ nights and cheap food.
- **Line:** *A que horas começa o concerto hoje?* — What time does the gig start tonight?
- **Meet:** The bar staff and the crowd at the long tables
- **Tip:** Check their agenda first. Eat in the front room, then move to the back for the music.
- **Room:** `lisbon_bilhetes`
- **Unconfirmed:** Current hours and prices not confirmed.
- **Sources:** https://www.timeout.pt/lisboa/pt/noite/damas · https://www.viralagenda.com/pt/p/DAMASLISBOA
- [x] verified (accepted by Sam, 2026-11-03)

### Micro Padaria — bakery

- **Where:** Rua Angelina Vidal 35A
- **Why:** A bakery a few square metres big, opened in 2018 by a former cell biologist — slow-fermented sourdough with a loyal street following, sold over the counter by the baker herself.
- **Line:** *Bom dia, qual é o pão de hoje?* — Good morning, what's today's bread?
- **Meet:** The owner-baker, at the counter
- **Tip:** Go in the morning — the good loaves sell out. Sweet things only some days, so ask.
- **Room:** `lisbon_padaria`
- **Unconfirmed:** Coordinates are the street segment; no 2025–26 review or confirmed hours; on the Graça/Arroios edge.
- **Sources:** https://www.timeout.pt/lisboa/pt/noticias/micro-padaria-pao-de-fermentacao-lenta-na-graca-em-meia-duzia-de-metros-quadrados-020318 · https://www.timeout.com/lisbon/restaurants/micro-padaria
- [x] verified (accepted by Sam, 2026-11-03)

### Jardim da Cerca da Graça — miradouro

- **Where:** Calçada do Monte 46
- **Why:** A big hillside garden opened in 2015, looking over Mouraria to the castle with hardly a tourist in it — and a kiosk baking cakes with produce from the community vegetable plots next door.
- **Line:** *Qual é o bolo do dia?* — What's the cake of the day?
- **Meet:** The kiosk staff, local families and dog walkers
- **Tip:** Take a coffee from the kiosk up to the top terrace at sunset — quieter than the Graça miradouro.
- **Room:** `lisbon_foto`
- **Unconfirmed:** Kiosk operator and hours for 2025–26 not confirmed; coordinates are the garden.
- **Sources:** https://www.timeout.pt/lisboa/pt/atraccoes/jardim-da-cerca-da-graca-1 · https://www.timeout.pt/lisboa/pt/restaurantes/quiosque-popular
- [x] verified (accepted by Sam, 2026-11-03)

## Mouraria & Intendente

Where fado was born and half the world now cooks; the most mixed square mile in the city.

### Zé dos Cornos — tasca

- **Where:** Beco dos Surradores 5
- **Why:** A tiny charcoal-grill tasca in an alley at the foot of Mouraria, named for the ox horns on its wall — long shared tables, strangers elbow to elbow, and pork ribs with bean rice.
- **Line:** *Importam-se que me sente aqui?* — Do you mind if I sit here?
- **Meet:** Your table neighbours, and the family on the grill
- **Tip:** No bookings — arrive before 12:30 or queue.
- **Room:** `lisbon_mesa`
- **Unconfirmed:** Hours and card payment conflict between sources.
- **Sources:** https://www.timeout.pt/lisboa/pt/restaurantes/ze-dos-cornos · https://www.2foodtrippers.com/ze-dos-cornos-lisbon/
- [x] verified (accepted by Sam, 2026-11-03)

### Cantinho do Aziz — tasca

- **Where:** Rua de São Lourenço 5
- **Why:** A Mozambican family restaurant since the 1980s — a reminder that Mouraria has always been the city's arrival neighbourhood. Matapa and prawn-and-crab makoufe in a plain, busy room.
- **Line:** *O que é que me aconselha hoje?* — What would you recommend today?
- **Meet:** The family and staff, who like explaining the dishes
- **Tip:** Ask about the lunch menu, which is not always posted. Book at weekends.
- **Room:** `lisbon_tasca`
- **Unconfirmed:** Hours conflict, including Sunday.
- **Sources:** https://www.timeout.pt/lisboa/pt/restaurantes/cantinho-do-aziz · https://www.timeout.com/lisbon/restaurants/cantinho-do-aziz
- [x] verified (accepted by Sam, 2026-11-03)

### A Vida Portuguesa (Intendente) — shop

- **Where:** Largo do Intendente Pina Manique 23
- **Why:** Long-running Portuguese brands — soaps, tins, enamelware, notebooks — in an old tiled ceramics warehouse. A good place to learn the names of everyday things, and to hear each brand's story.
- **Line:** *É para oferta, pode embrulhar?* — It's a gift — could you wrap it?
- **Meet:** The staff at the counter
- **Tip:** Afterwards, a drink on the Largo do Intendente outside.
- **Room:** `lisbon_conta`
- **Unconfirmed:** A small chain, though this is its flagship.
- **Sources:** https://www.avidaportuguesa.com/en/stores/intendente · https://www.timeout.pt/lisboa/pt/compras/a-vida-portuguesa
- [x] verified (accepted by Sam, 2026-11-03)

### Maria da Mouraria — venue

- **Where:** Largo da Severa 2
- **Why:** The neighbourhood's own fado house, in the building said to be where Maria Severa — the singer at the root of fado's legend — spent her last years. Away from the Alfama strip.
- **Line:** *A que horas começa o fado?* — What time does the fado start?
- **Meet:** The host, and the fadistas and guitarists between sets
- **Tip:** Book for dinner and keep silent during the sets. Look at the tiny Largo da Severa on the way in.
- **Room:** `lisbon_fado`
- **Unconfirmed:** Current hours and prices not confirmed.

## Alfama

Away from the castle queue: stairways, washing lines, and neighbours who talk across them.

### A Baiuca — venue

- **Where:** Rua de São Miguel 20
- **Why:** A stone-walled room of about thirty seats where fado vadio happens — neighbours and amateurs take turns over dinner, backed by the house musicians. It feels like walking into a family party.
- **Line:** *Ainda há lugar para duas pessoas?* — Is there still room for two?
- **Meet:** The owners, the amateur singers, the regulars
- **Tip:** Book — it is tiny — and go hungry: there is a minimum spend. Silence during each fado; tip the musicians.
- **Room:** `lisbon_fado`
- **Unconfirmed:** Schedule and minimum spend from undated guides; no 2025–26 confirmation.
- **Sources:** https://theeuropeanbarguide.com/tag/wine/ · https://cdn.fodors.com/world/europe/portugal/lisbon/neighborhoods/alfama/things-to-do/nightlife
- [x] verified (accepted by Sam, 2026-11-03)

### Pateo 13 — tasca

- **Where:** Calçadinha de Santo Estêvão 13
- **Why:** A courtyard grill hidden in Alfama's back steps: sardines and fish off the charcoal at trestle tables, among neighbours rather than tour groups.
- **Line:** *Uma dose de sardinhas, se faz favor.* — A portion of sardines, please.
- **Meet:** The grill man, and the staff taking orders table to table
- **Tip:** Arrive before 19:00 for a table, and order salada de pimentos with the sardines.
- **Room:** `lisbon_mesa`
- **Unconfirmed:** Hours conflict; may be seasonal or quieter in winter.
- **Sources:** https://www.timeout.pt/lisboa/pt/restaurantes/pateo-13 · https://lisboasecreta.co/en/grilled-sardines-in-lisbon-en/
- [x] verified (accepted by Sam, 2026-11-03)

### Miradouro de Santo Estêvão — miradouro

- **Where:** Largo de Santo Estêvão
- **Why:** A small terrace by the Santo Estêvão church at the top of steep stairs, which most visitors never find — Alfama's rooftops to the river, and residents on the benches.
- **Line:** *Desculpe, o miradouro é por aqui?* — Excuse me, is the viewpoint this way?
- **Meet:** Residents on the benches, and whoever you ask on the way up
- **Tip:** Get lost on purpose and ask the way. Pateo 13 is just below.
- **Room:** `lisbon_perdido`
- **Unconfirmed:** nothing flagged
- **Sources:** https://getlisbon.com/discovering/discreet-viewpoints-lisbon/ · https://www.portugalvisitor.com/lisbon-churches/igreja-de-santo-estevao

- **Line:** *Hoje há fado à tarde?* — Is there fado this afternoon?
- **Meet:** Members behind the bar, and the older Alfama regulars
- **Tip:** Ask at the bar for fado days. In June, come for the club's arraial.
- **Room:** `lisbon_ola`
- **Unconfirmed:** Coordinates approximate (not in OSM); current fado schedule not confirmed.

### Fábrica do Pastel de Feijão — bakery

- **Where:** Rua dos Remédios 33
- **Why:** A small pastry shop on Alfama's working high street baking the pastel de feijão — a bean-and-almond tart from Torres Vedras — for a change from the nata.
- **Line:** *Um pastel de feijão e uma bica, por favor.* — A bean tart and an espresso, please.
- **Meet:** The person at the counter
- **Tip:** Ask what's in it — 'Leva o quê?'. The pastry won EU protected status in 2025, a good opener.

## Campo de Ourique

A village inside the city: a market hall, the last stop of the 28, and nobody in a hurry.

### Mercado de Campo de Ourique — market

- **Where:** Rua Coelho da Rocha 104
- **Why:** The neighbourhood's own market: fresh fruit, fish, meat and flower stalls in the morning for people who live here, and a food-court side that fills up for lunch and evening petiscos.
- **Line:** *Quanto custa o quilo das laranjas?* — How much is a kilo of oranges?
- **Meet:** The stallholders at the fresh-produce bancas
- **Tip:** Weekday morning for the fresh stalls; after dark for the food counters and a glass of wine.
- **Room:** `lisbon_mercado`
- **Unconfirmed:** Fresh-stall hours vary by source (about Mon–Sat 7:00–14:00); food-court hours run late.
- **Sources:** https://www.openstreetmap.org/search?query=Mercado%20de%20Campo%20de%20Ourique · https://observador.pt/especiais/gastronomicos-e-culturais-serao-assim-os-mercados-futuro/ · https://doeatbetterexperience.com/blog/mercado-de-campo-de-ourique/
- [x] verified (accepted by Sam, 2026-11-03)

### Pastelaria Aloma — bakery

- **Where:** Rua Francisco Metrass 67
- **Why:** A neighbourhood bakery since 1943, listed as a Loja com História. Its pastel de nata has won the city's best-nata contest, yet the street still drops in for one and a bica at the counter.
- **Line:** *Queria dois pastéis de nata, por favor.* — I'd like two custard tarts, please.
- **Meet:** The person behind the counter
- **Tip:** Eat it at the balcão while it is warm — they will ask if you want canela on top.
- **Room:** `lisbon_padaria`
- **Unconfirmed:** Opening hours vary by source; number of contest wins varies.
- **Sources:** https://www.timeout.pt/lisboa/pt/restaurantes/pastelaria-aloma · https://www.spottedbylocals.com/lisbon/pastelaria-aloma · https://lojascomhistoria.pt/shops/pastelaria-aloma
- [x] verified (accepted by Sam, 2026-11-03)

### Imperial de Campo de Ourique — tasca

- **Where:** Rua Correia Teles 67–69
- **Why:** A tavern since 1947 and a Loja com História, cooking the north of Portugal — cozido, chanfana, bacalhau à minhota — with the day's dishes chalked on a slate carried from table to table.
- **Line:** *Qual é o prato do dia hoje?* — What's the dish of the day today?
- **Meet:** The host who brings the slate to your table
- **Tip:** A lunch place (about Mon–Sat until 16:00); the goat dishes are said to be Fridays and Saturdays.
- **Room:** `lisbon_tasca`
- **Unconfirmed:** No 2025–26 confirmation it is still open; newest review found is 2021. Host's name (Sr. João) not confirmed recently.

### Garrafeira Campo de Ourique — shop

- **Where:** Rua Tomás da Anunciação 29A
- **Why:** A neighbourhood wine shop where you say what you are cooking and what you want to spend, and they choose the bottle — numbers and tastes, out loud.
- **Line:** *Que tinto me recomenda até quinze euros?* — Which red would you recommend for up to fifteen euros?
- **Meet:** The shopkeeper
- **Tip:** Give a budget and a dish — 'para acompanhar peixe' — and let them choose. Closed on Sundays.
- **Room:** `lisbon_supermercado`
- **Unconfirmed:** No dated 2025–26 review; hours vary by source.

### Casa Fernando Pessoa — other

- **Where:** Rua Coelho da Rocha 16–18
- **Why:** The house where Pessoa lived his last fifteen years, now a small literary house with a poetry library and readings — a quiet place where the language itself is the point.
- **Line:** *Um bilhete, por favor. Há desconto para estudantes?* — One ticket, please. Is there a student discount?
- **Meet:** The staff at the front desk, and whoever comes to the readings
- **Tip:** Look for a free reading or talk on the agenda, then walk two minutes to the market.
- **Room:** `lisbon_bilhetes`
- **Unconfirmed:** Library closed for works 1 Jul 2025 – 2 Jan 2026; reopening not confirmed.
- **Sources:** https://www.timeout.pt/lisboa/pt/atraccoes/casa-fernando-pessoa · https://www.casafernandopessoa.pt/en/cfp/news-publications/noticias/library-casa-fernando-pessoa-temporary-closure

## Arroios & Anjos

The neighbourhood with the most nationalities in Portugal, and the cafés to match.

### Mercado de Arroios — market

- **Where:** Rua Ângela Pinto
- **Why:** A municipal market in a modernist building, renovated and still where residents buy meat, fish and vegetables — and where the neighbourhood holds its makers' markets.
- **Line:** *Pode cortar isto em bifes finos, por favor?* — Could you cut this into thin steaks, please?
- **Meet:** The butcher and the fishmonger at their stalls
- **Tip:** Go in the morning when the stalls are full; if a word escapes you, point and ask for 'meio quilo'.
- **Room:** `lisbon_talho`
- **Unconfirmed:** Hours not confirmed; current stall list not seen.
- **Sources:** https://www.timeout.pt/lisboa/pt/noticias/com-pequenos-grandes-criadores-e-produtores-faz-se-o-mercado-da-primavera-em-arroios-031426 · https://www.trienaldelisboa.com/ohl/en/espaco/mercado-de-arroios/
- [x] verified (accepted by Sam, 2026-11-03)

### Adega do Domingos — tasca

- **Where:** Rua de Arroios 56-A
- **Why:** A family-run adega with an open kitchen and tables close together — francesinha, pataniscas, picanha in portions built for sharing, and a room of families and regulars rather than visitors.
- **Line:** *Uma dose dá para duas pessoas?* — Is one portion enough for two people?
- **Meet:** The family serving the tables, and the regulars at the next one
- **Tip:** Portions are big — regulars say two dishes feed three. Book on busy days.
- **Room:** `lisbon_mesa`
- **Unconfirmed:** Street number and hours from OSM only.
- **Sources:** https://www.nit.pt/comida/restaurantes/a-tasca-de-arroios-com-francesinhas-e-doses-para-farta-brutos · https://www.openstreetmap.org/search?query=Adega%20do%20Domingos%20Lisboa

- **Where:** Rua do Forno do Tijolo 71A

- **Meet:** Sr. Júlio Pais and his daughter
- **Tip:** Walk in on a weekday morning and expect to talk while you wait.

## Marvila & Beato

Old warehouses by the river turned breweries, galleries and studios — Lisbon’s east, still being found.

### Dois Corvos Taproom — bar

- **Where:** Rua Capitão Leitão 94
- **Why:** The original taproom of one of Lisbon's first craft breweries, in Marvila's old warehouse streets — beer from the tanks, poured by people who will talk you through each one.
- **Line:** *Pode trazer a conta, se faz favor?* — Could you bring the bill, please?
- **Meet:** The bartenders, who know the brewery inside out
- **Tip:** Order the tasting tray to try the range. Opens mid-afternoon.
- **Room:** `lisbon_conta`
- **Unconfirmed:** Monday opening conflicts between sources.
- **Sources:** https://doiscorvos.pt/-/visit/taproom-marvila · https://www.timeout.pt/lisboa/pt/noticias/dois-corvos-celebra-dez-anos-com-festa-em-marvila-091025 · https://mesprestiges.com/en/lisboa/places/dois-corvos-marvila
- [x] verified (accepted by Sam, 2026-11-03)

### Fábrica Braço de Prata — venue

- **Where:** Rua da Fábrica de Material de Guerra 1
- **Why:** A former munitions factory turned rambling cultural house — rooms of books, jazz and samba nights, readings and film, and fairs where Portuguese is spoken in accents from both sides of the Atlantic.
- **Line:** *Quanto é a entrada hoje à noite?* — How much is entry tonight?
- **Meet:** The people on the door and at the bar, the musicians, the other guests
- **Tip:** Go on a weekend night and wander between rooms; many nights are pay-what-you-can.
- **Room:** `lisbon_bilhetes`
- **Unconfirmed:** Regular opening days not confirmed; bookshop rooms not confirmed in 2025 sources.
- **Sources:** https://www.timeout.pt/lisboa/pt/noticias/fabrica-do-braco-de-prata-celebra-18-anos-com-noite-de-musica-e-resistencia-061325 · https://dnbrasil.dn.pt/feira-brasileira-da-fabrica-braco-de-prata-tera-edicao-especial-nordeste/ · https://ma.to/event/africa-day-sound-system-24-may-2026
- [x] verified (accepted by Sam, 2026-11-03)

### Galeria Underdogs — other

- **Where:** Rua Fernando Palha, Armazém 56
- **Why:** An urban-art gallery in a Poço do Bispo warehouse with artist residencies open to the public — and its artists' murals on walls all round the area.
- **Line:** *Posso tirar fotografias às obras?* — Can I take photos of the works?
- **Meet:** The gallery staff, and sometimes the artists in residence
- **Tip:** Open Tue–Sat afternoons. Afterwards, walk the nearby streets for the murals.

### Varanda do Vale Formoso — tasca

- **Where:** Rua do Vale Formoso de Cima 113
- **Why:** A modest place on a street known for snail houses, squeezed between a lane, a fast road and the railway — caracóis, bacalhau à Brás, bitoque and a cold beer.
